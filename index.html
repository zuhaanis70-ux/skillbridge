Responsible Use

SkillBridge supports human decision-making rather than replacing it.

Human oversight

Recommendations are suggestions only. Managers and project leads make the final staffing decision.

Explainability

Matches are based on understandable evidence such as required skills, experience, availability and previous project evidence.

Privacy and fairness

The demonstration uses anonymised sample workforce data. Sensitive personal attributes should not be used for staffing recommendations, and employee information should only be accessed for legitimate organisational purposes.
