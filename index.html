Responsible Use & Bias Controls

These controls are part of the capstone design, not an optional add-on.

Human review
Recommendations support decisions; they do not automatically assign employees.
Explainability
Every recommendation shows matching skills, experience and relevant project evidence.
Data minimisation
The MVP avoids sensitive personal attributes and uses work-related capability data.
Profile correction
Employees should be able to review and correct inaccurate capability information.
Availability fairness
Availability is used as a staffing constraint, not as a measure of employee value.
Bias monitoring
Managers should periodically review recommendations for systematic exclusion or over-reliance on past project history.
