How SkillBridge Works

1. Describe the need

A manager describes the skills and experience required for a project.

2. Search expertise

SkillBridge searches employee skills, experience, availability and project history.

3. Explain recommendations

Each recommendation includes matching skills, evidence and potential gaps.

4. Human makes the decision

The platform supports decision-making but does not automatically select employees.

Responsible Use & Governance

SkillBridge is designed as a decision-support system rather than an automated hiring or staffing decision-maker.

Human review

Recommendations are advisory. A manager or authorised human makes the final staffing decision.

Explainability

Recommendations show matching skills, experience, project evidence and potential gaps.

Data minimisation

The demo uses anonymised professional information rather than sensitive personal data.

Profile correction

Employees should be able to review and correct their skills, experience and availability information.

Availability fairness

Availability is used as a staffing constraint, not as a measure of employee value or capability.

Bias monitoring

Recommendations should be periodically reviewed for systematic differences across departments, locations and other relevant groups.
